 Bill Management System

 Problem Statement
Manual handling of bills may be tedious and error prone while calculating and storing the records.

The software named Bill Management System will allow the user to add customer details, add product details, calculate the amount of the bill and the Goods and Service Tax (GST), generate bills and store them for future references which will ease the process.

Scope

The scope of the project is to develop a software which will allow the user to

- enter the customer's details
- add the product's details
- calculate the amount of the each product
- calculate the total amount and the GST

- generate the bill
- save the bill in the SQLite database
- view the previous bills
- view the basic analytics of the billing
  Target Users
- Shop owners
- Retail stores
- Small scale businesses
- Students
- Anyone who requires a basic billing system
 High-Level Features
- Customer management
- Product management
- Automatic calculation of bills
- Calculation of Goods and Service Tax (GST)
- Generation of bills
- Storage in SQLite database
- History of bills
- Basic analytics
- Graphical User Interface (GUI)
